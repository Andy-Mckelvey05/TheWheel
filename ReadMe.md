# Wheel
The wheel is a community event that allows people to have a chance to share films with others.

## Info

### What Is The Wheel?
Each participant lists _X_ films, each one of these films is put into a randomized wheel.
The film selected by the wheel is removed unless skipped, this is repeated until one film remains. We all then watch the remaining film.

### What Is A Skip?
Once per wheel, a participant can stop a film being removed by 'skipping' it, this will save it from that spin only, if a skip has not been used by the last spin it will be used automatically.

### New Comer Bonus
If its your first ever wheel, you get 2 skips, you are also exempt from the 15min rule.

# Rules

## 15min Rule
The wheel host can trigger a 15min timer, this is a hard cut off for film selection, any slots not filled in when the timer runs out will be lost.

## Repeat Rule
A film that wins the wheel is banned for the rest of the calendar year to encourage variety. The list of films that have won in the current year can be seen [here](https://github.com/Andy-Mckelvey05/TheWheel/blob/main/Banned%20Movies.txt).

## Sequel Rule
A sequel film cannot be put on the wheel until its predecessors have been seen by the wheel, this is not year bound (e.g. if `Sonic the Hedgehog` is watched dec 2025, `Sonic the Hedgehog 2` is still valid jan 2026, even though `Sonic the Hedgehog` became valid again with the new year)

These are usually treated on a case by case basis (e.g. release order or chronological order?), these are some examples to help inform decisions.

- Two films may share a universe as long as they remain mostly unrelated.
    - for example `Puss in Boots` does not require `Shrek` but `Puss in Boots: The Last Wish` will require `Puss in Boots`
    - Another example of this is most Marvel Cinematic Universe films. 

- TV tie in films are valid, as there is no way to watch the show through the wheel.
    - For example `The Simpsons Movie`, or `El Camino: A Breaking Bad Movie`

# Events

### What Are Events?
Events are special versions of the wheel that take place for special occasions. Events may have their own rules or addons that differ from the normal wheel.

## HalloWheel!

- During October only spookie films can be put on the wheel.
- A spookie film is any horror, or just any film that is scary or creepy.
- `Repeat Rule` still applies.
